About Marquis

A small spa for a fuller kind of attention.

Marquis day SPA brings together a forest setting, sensory care, and curiosity about how we experience the body.

Created by Daniel McShan

Marquis is a private, forest-surrounded spa created as an antidote to overstimulation and endless screens. Its rituals are designed around the whole atmosphere: light, sound, temperature, touch, and the pace of the room.

Rather than chasing trends, Marquis brings together a considered set of sensory and reflective experiences. Some are quiet and restorative; others invite exploration and play. Each begins with a conversation so the session can be shaped around your preferences and boundaries.

Curiosity with care

The spa’s approach is science-aware and experiential. It is not a medical clinic or psychotherapy practice, and its sessions do not diagnose or treat health conditions. When a ritual raises a health question, consult an appropriately qualified health professional before scheduling.

The experience is collaborative. You set the intention, name your limits, and stay in charge of your participation throughout the session.
