Frequently asked questions

Is Marquis a medical or therapy practice?

No. Marquis is a spa and experiential wellness space. Sessions do not diagnose or treat medical or mental-health conditions and are not a substitute for licensed care.

Can I change my mind during a session?

Yes. You may ask to pause, change direction, or end a session at any point. Boundaries are discussed before a ritual begins.

Does submitting the form confirm my appointment?

No. It records a booking request and preferred time. Wait for personal confirmation before treating the time as booked.

Where is the spa?

Marquis is in a private forest setting. Location and visit details are shared after a session is confirmed.

What does a session cost?

Current service cards do not show reliable pricing. Confirm the price and session details directly before agreeing to a booking.

Choose comfort over momentum.
You do not need to share more than you want to. Please keep booking-form notes general and avoid including sensitive personal or health information.
